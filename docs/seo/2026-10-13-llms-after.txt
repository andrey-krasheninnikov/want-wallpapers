# Want Wallpapers

> A public gallery of desktop and mobile wallpapers with original PNG downloads.

Usage license:

The wallpapers are free to keep on your own screens.

You may:

Download and use the original images as personal wallpapers on your devices, including a personal computer, phone or tablet. You may crop them for your own screen.

Please do not:

Sell, redistribute or upload the images as a wallpaper pack; use them in advertising, products or other commercial work; or claim them as your own work. Ask wallpapers@want.foundation for any use beyond personal, noncommercial display.

Ownership:

The images remain the property of their rights holder. This license grants limited use, not ownership.
