# Electric Gallery

The gallery places wallpapers on neutral graphite surfaces. Saturated violet marks actions and selected states. Soft white text and neutral supporting text keep reading comfortable. Filled surfaces distinguish the page, a panel and its controls. Artwork retains its own colors.

One softly blurred violet sphere sits behind the images on a solid graphite ground. It stays near the upper-right edge and drifts over thirty-six seconds at constant low opacity. Visitors can stop the motion, and the site remembers that choice. Reduced motion keeps the background still; forced colors remove the decoration.

Compact Onest headings introduce each section. Manrope carries body text and controls, while system monospace distinguishes small indices and metadata. Stories, formats, licensing and topics stay in measured reading columns. Legal explanations continue inside the same reading surface.

A writing surface holds the feedback form; linked rows hold contact actions; a compact feed holds comments. Corners follow the size and purpose of the surface. Button focus changes fill and text together. Fields and the viewer keep dark fills with a small keyboard focus outline. Colored halos do not surround controls or cards.

Text and panels appear immediately. Controls give short feedback, and neighboring wallpapers have a brief directional transition. Video covers and the device scene start only after a press. Videos return to a poster when out of view and wait for another press. The background sphere never moves content or intercepts input. The visual pass checks contrast over the sphere, translated wrapping, focus and alignment at desktop and mobile sizes.
